# PM — landing-and-support-chat (2026-10-23)
**Problem:** a visitor at the root URL only sees a sign-in form. There's no explanation, no prices and no way to ask a question.
**Users:** jastip sellers evaluating Bawa (solo sellers and small teams), plus Kenny as platform owner answering them.
**User stories**
1. As a visitor, I understand in 10 seconds what Bawa does and who it's for.
2. As a visitor, I can see what the app looks like with realistic example data, clearly labelled as an example.
3. As a visitor, I see the plan prices and limits, and that billing is a manual invoice.
4. As a visitor, I can ask a question without an account and see the reply in the same chat later.
5. As the platform owner, I see new chats in the admin console, reply, close or block them, and copy the contact details to follow up.
**Success:** sign-up clicks from the landing page; chats answered within a day; no spam floods (rate limits hold).
**Non-goals:** merchant in-app chat, owner notifications, attachments, realtime.
**Copy rules:** sentence case, friendly and short; no invented testimonials, logos or user counts.
